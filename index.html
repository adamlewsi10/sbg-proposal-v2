Two Routes Forward

Since we last met, we’ve explored the question of scope more deeply.

Here’s where we’ve landed.

Option A · Recommended

Marketing Hub Enterprise + Dynamics 365

Move quickly. Solve the marketing pain. Keep Dynamics where it is.

What it is

HubSpot Marketing Hub Enterprise becomes your marketing engagement layer, integrated with your existing Dynamics 365 CRM. Dynamics remains the operational system of record for firms, contacts, opportunities, contracts and servicing.

What it solves

  • Marketing self-service — segmentation, email build, journeys at speed
  • Multi-brand architecture (Sesame, Bankhall, PMS) via Business Units
  • Lead capture, scoring and handoff to Dynamics
  • Event marketing consolidation
  • First-ever marketing attribution and ROI reporting
Timeframe
MVP in weeks, full rollout in months
Risk profile
Lower — targeted, integration-led
Reusable in Option B. Everything we’d build in Option A is reusable in Option B. None of the work gets thrown away.
Option B · Future-State

Full HubSpot Platform Consolidation

A longer-term simplification of the full estate.

What it is

HubSpot progressively replaces Dynamics 365 across sales, service and marketing. A simpler, SaaS-first architecture aligned around a single platform.

What it would address

  • Account / contact identity model (the duplicate-individual problem)
  • Business Unit permissions and brand governance
  • Contracts, products, price lists, entitlements
  • Power Automate logic and integration redesign
  • Customer portals, MIFAS legacy dependencies, OneView roadmap
  • Data platform integration and downstream feeds
Timeframe
Multi-phase transformation programme
Risk profile
Higher — needs architectural validation
Phase 1 of Option B is essentially Option A. Option B would always be delivered in phases — and the first phase looks much like Option A.
Our Recommendation

Option A. It solves the immediate marketing pain quickly, with no wasted work — everything we build is reusable in Option B if you later decide to go further.

Option A in Detail

What does Option A look like?

Two phases, two workstreams, one platform. Phase 1 delivers an MVP — your team sending email marketing from HubSpot. Phase 2 rolls out the full marketing engine. Both workstreams run in parallel within each phase.

Phase 1

MVP — Email Marketing Live

Milestone: First campaign sent from HubSpot

Data & Platform

  • HubSpot portal setup and Business Unit configuration (x4 brands)
  • Data model design — properties, associations, lifecycle stages
  • Contact data migration from D365 (cleansing, deduplication, mapping)
  • Multi-brand consent and preference centre architecture
  • D365 bi-directional integration (native sync or middleware)
  • User provisioning, permissions, and security

Marketing Activation

  • Brand email templates designed and built per brand
  • Core segmentation lists (weekly newsletters, regulatory, partner comms)
  • Transactional email setup for regulatory “must read” comms
  • Initial automation workflows (welcome, event registration)
  • Marketing team training and onboarding
  • First campaign: plan, build, send, report
Phase 2

Full Rollout — Marketing Engine at Scale

Milestone: Full omnichannel marketing + events

Data & Platform

  • OneView API integration scoping and build
  • Data governance framework and naming conventions
  • Advanced reporting datasets and custom properties
  • Lead scoring and engagement scoring models
  • Integration monitoring and error handling

Marketing Activation

  • Event management rollout + BBD Boom event module deployment
  • Social media publishing, inbox, and ads integration
  • WhatsApp channel activation
  • Campaign and attribution reporting dashboards
  • AB testing framework and smart content
  • AI brand voice setup per brand
  • Advanced automation (nurture sequences, event lifecycle, partner comms)

Option B in Detail

What does Option B look like?

Before any commitment to a full platform consolidation, Option B starts with a System Design & Technical Discovery Sprint — resolving the architecture questions that determine whether broader Dynamics replacement is the right move.

Engagement

The sprint covers nine workstreams:

01

Marketing Operating Model

Confirm HubSpot Marketing Hub scope across email, journeys, forms, lists, reporting, social and events.

02

Brand & Consent Design

Define Business Units, subscription types, preference centres, asset separation, opt-in/out behaviour.

03

Dynamics Data Model Review

Identify objects and fields required for HubSpot phase one.

04

Contact Identity Design

Decide how duplicate individuals, email addresses, brands and firm relationships should be represented.

05

Integration Design

Validate native Dynamics integration vs iPaaS or custom approach.

06

Lead Lifecycle & Handoff

Define lead capture, scoring, qualification and sync to Dynamics.

07

Event Architecture

Determine which events move into HubSpot and whether CVent remains for large conferences.

08

Reporting & Attribution

Define campaign, lead and revenue attribution requirements.

09

Future CRM Migration Assessment

Identify what would be required to migrate sales and service from Dynamics to HubSpot.
